# tenantbleed brochures

Two-page A4 brochure for founders and agencies shipping on Supabase: one message, three benefits with one proof point each, three steps, call to action. Technical detail lives in the product docs, not here.

To make a PDF: open the HTML in Chrome, Print, Save as PDF, A4, margins None, background graphics on. Replace `[contact]` before sending. tenantbleed is free and open source: there is no pricing to show.

`tenantbleed-technical-brief.html` is the companion for technical buyers (CTOs, security leads, agency tech leads): what a scan does, what it attacks and when a finding counts as proven, our test results, linter triage, tested fixes, setup, safety and limits. Two A4 pages, same PDF steps.
